Solar Panel Maintenance Guide

Proper maintenance keeps your solar system efficient for 25+ years. In Nepal's dusty/monsoon climate, regular care is especially important.

Recommended Maintenance Schedule

  • Cleaning: 3–6 times per year (more in dusty Terai or Kathmandu valley areas). Use soft brush, mild soap & water — never abrasive materials or hard water that leaves spots.
  • Visual Inspection: Monthly or after strong winds/monsoon — check for dust buildup, bird droppings, cracks, discoloration, or animal damage.
  • Check Mounting & Wiring: Every 6 months — ensure no loose bolts, corroded connections, or damaged cables (especially important before monsoon).
  • Inverter & Battery Care: Quarterly — clean vents/fans, check display for errors, ensure batteries (if off-grid) are topped up and ventilated.
  • Performance Monitoring: Daily/weekly via inverter app or online portal — sudden drop in output usually means cleaning or shading issue.
  • Professional Service: Once every 1–2 years (or if errors appear) — full system check by certified technician.

Estimated Costs in Nepal (2026)

  • DIY cleaning: Almost free (just water + time)
  • Professional cleaning (3–5 kW system): NPR 3,000–7,000 per visit
  • Annual full maintenance contract: NPR 8,000–18,000 depending on system size
  • Replacement parts (rare): Inverter fan ~NPR 5,000–15,000; battery (if used) every 5–8 years

Tip: Many installers in Nepal now offer annual maintenance packages at low cost — ask when buying the system.
